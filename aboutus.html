About Us

Nature Beauty is driven by the belief that everyone
deserves to experience the incredible benefits of nature.
We have explored the world to source the finest
natural ingredients, constantly pushing boundaries
to discover even more. Through our meticulous
craftsmanship, we have created health-conscious essentials
for the skin, hair, body, and spirit, offering a truly
luxurious organic experience.Nature Beauty is
registered in Bangladesh and incorporated under the Companies.

Our Journey

Love Beauty and Planet was started by a group of like-minded people who
       believe that looking good and doing good should go hand-in-hand.

Our Story

At Love Beauty and Planet, we are driven by our purpose to craft high-
performing hair and body care products that are as kind to the planet as
they are to your hair and skin. Our efficacious formulas are made with
potent natural ingredients, without any baddies, bottled in planet-positive
packaging. We believe that these small acts of love can make a big
difference in creating a harmonious world for generations to come.

High Performing Products

At Love Beauty and Planet, we are dedicated to crafting high-performing
products powered by potent natural ingredients that solve your haircare
and skincare concerns. We handpick these ingredients from around the
world to ensure you get the best of nature. Take, for example, our Olive
Oil Peptide Bond Repair range, which harnesses the power of wheat
protein and peptides that deeply penetrate hair fibres and repair
damage from within.

Uplifting Sensorials

Carefully crafted to elevate your daily beauty routine, our ranges are
infused with their own signature scent, derived from 100% natural,
ethically-sourced essential oils. From the calming aroma of handpicked
French lavender to a zesty burst of Italian mandarin oil, there’s a note
you’ll surely love as it lingers on your hair and skin through the day. Our
decadent product textures are also a treat to your senses – as you lather
up with our creamy shampoos and lightweight body gels, you’ll notice
that each one is designed to indulge you in a moment of pure bliss.
